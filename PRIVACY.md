# Privacy Policy for fluffyGram

**Last Updated: May 15, 2026**

fluffyGram ("we", "our", or "us") is an unofficial Telegram client. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the fluffyGram mobile application.

## 1. Core Service
fluffyGram is a fork of the official Telegram for Android application and uses the Telegram API to provide its services. By using fluffyGram, you are also agreeing to the [Telegram Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy).

## 2. Information We Collect
**fluffyGram does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or any other tracking services that collect your personal information.
- **No Remote Servers:** All communication is performed directly between your device and Telegram's official servers. We do not operate any intermediate servers that process your messages or metadata.
- **Local Logs:** If enabled by the user for debugging purposes, logs are stored locally on your device and are never automatically transmitted to us.

## 3. App Actions
Our application uses Android App Actions to provide shortcuts for common tasks (like starting a call or searching). These actions are processed locally by the Android system and do not involve any data collection by fluffyGram.

## 4. Open Source
The source code for fluffyGram is public and available for audit at [https://github.com/krolchonok/fluffyGram](https://github.com/krolchonok/fluffyGram).

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via GitHub Issues in the repository.
